# Rapier as the body: the evidence

What the checks of the Rapier-body work (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-rapier-body-ai-brain-design.md`, lane one: the galaxy surface) found, kept so a later lane can see what the body cost and looked like before the next world moved onto it.

- `cost.json`: `npx vitest run --config vitest.ai.config.js src/lib/physics/cost.scenario.test.js`, the body’s cost in Node against the real engine on this container’s CPU (not a browser’s frame): twenty figures on the character controller with their ten hurtboxes each, forty fixed and forty loose props on four heightfield tiles, the brains’ queries at the mid tier’s budget (a sight ray each a frame, three whiskers and a ledge probe for the fifth due), six hundred frames. The design’s table asked for 2.9 ms a frame on the mid tier across the step, the moves, the queries and the sync; the measure is 2.06 ms, the step 1.55 of it (the twenty moves 1.06), the queries 0.33, the hurtboxes’ placing 0.17. The sweeps ran over their budget of 8 on a frame where four figures were due (12 asked, 4 refused, each keeping its last answer), which is the budget doing its job.
- `hurtboxes-endor.jpg` and `hurtboxes-endor.json`: `OUT=docs/superpowers/evidence/rapier-body node scripts/hurtbox-sheet.mjs endor` through the dev server, headless Chromium on software WebGL: the world opened, its first quest with something to shoot begun (`bunker`: six rigged troopers at the bunker), you set down beside them (a figure far from you sleeps, its rig with it), their hurtboxes asked for as wire (the scene’s `hurtboxes(true)` dev hook, `lib/three/combat/hitboxRig.js`’s `debug`: six rigs), the view held on the first, and a frame’s counters (four sight rays of a budget of sixteen, three substeps, 1,200 bodies) with each figure’s mind (`patrol`). The picture shows a trooper by the bunker and no wire over it: `hitboxRig.test.js` proves the wire meshes follow the bones in Node, but their drawing in the page was not seen on this container’s software renderer, and the probe that would have said where they sat was killed for memory. Tatooine’s and Hoth’s first quests spawn inside a zone or not at once, so their sheets showed no figure and were not kept. The sheet in a real browser is the next lane’s check.
- The smoke (`node scripts/autopilot-check.mjs --only smoke --skip lint,test,build --routes /galaxy/tatooine/surface,/galaxy/hoth/surface`) was green after the player’s body (Task 13), the hostiles’ (Task 14) and the hits (Task 15): a canvas drew, no console error.
- `scripts/perf-probe.mjs surface` was run too, on this container’s software WebGL: its frame times (3 to 13 fps, dominated by the software rasteriser and asset loading) say nothing about the body, which is why the cost is measured in Node. A run on a real chip is the next lane’s first task.
