# Fleet War Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. This plan is executed inline (executing-plans): the user asked for no subagents.

**Goal:** Battlefront 2 Fleet Assault battles at a front each crew can move, rocks that hurt at super speed, and a coronal mass ejection that looks like one.

**Architecture:** The rules are pure modules, tested in Node:
- `rockHits.js`: rocks
- `cme.js`'s timeline: the ejection
- `wars.js`, `war.js` and `battle.js`: the war and its battles

Thin three.js modules draw them:
- the ejection in `cme.js`
- `battleScene.js`: the battle
- `front.js`: the front's lifecycle, its beacon and the save

`scene.js` wires each in at the few places the skirmish and the meteors already use: the shot loop, the lock's candidates, the director's `busy` and the step after `state.ship = ship`.

**Tech Stack:**
- three 0.186
- React 19
- vitest 5
- playwright-core, for the browser checks
- @gltf-transform/meshoptimizer, for the imports

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-06-fleet-war-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- British spelling, curly quotes in prose and UI copy, plain sentences; comments say why.
- No sequel trilogy (Episodes 7–9) anywhere in Star Wars content.
- Rules go in pure tested modules, apart from the drawing. Tests are written first.
- No runtime calls to asset services. Models are downloaded or generated ahead, committed, and credited in `src/data/modelCredits.json`.
- Never print or commit `MESHY_API_KEY` or `SKETCHFAB_API_TOKEN`.
- Every scene starts from `lib/device`'s tier, loads only when near, and disposes on leave.
- Textures: WebP, 2K at most on desktop.
- Fighters a side by tier: `high` 32, `mid` 20, `low` 10.
- Tickets 150 a side. Battle clock 720 s. Rock damage at most 45 a hit.
- Commit messages are one plain sentence. Every commit ends with the session's attribution lines.
- Before every push: `npm run lint`, `npm test` and `npm run build`, all clean.

## Review Focus

1. **A rock hit while the autopilot flies on super speed.** The autopilot must not ram the same field again and again. Covered by the hit cooldown and the hidden rock (Task A2, test `a rock once hit is skipped while hidden`).
2. **Leaving the battle zone mid-fight, then coming back.** The battle resumes where it was and doesn't respawn a second fleet (Task B6, test `leaving pauses and coming back resumes the same battle`).
3. **A corrupt or old `tp-war-*` save.** The war falls back to a fresh one, without throwing (Task B2, test `a bad save loads as a new war`).
4. **The player dies mid-battle.** The scene's crash and respawn path runs as ever. Enemies drop the dead player as a target (Task B3, test `a dead player is not targeted`).
5. **Two flagships at once, or the defender losing all its fighters.** Only the defender's flagship carries objectives, and a team on zero tickets stops respawning (Task B3, tests `only the defender's flagship has objectives` and `no respawn without tickets`).

### Task 8 (B3): `battle.js`, the Fleet Assault sim

**Files:**
- Create: `src/components/universe/battle.js`
- Test: `src/components/universe/battle.test.js`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `WARS`, `FIGHTERS`, `SUBSYSTEMS`, `TURRETS` (B1); `sweptHit` from `targeting.js`.
- Produces `BATTLE`:
  - `{ radius: 150, lines: 90, tickets: 150, clock: 720, respawn: [3, 6] }`
  - bolts: `{ laser: { speed: 60, life: 0.6, damage: 1 }, flak: { speed: 70, life: 0.35, damage: 0.5 }, turbo: { speed: 45, life: 3.2, damage: 6 }, torpedo: { speed: 18, life: 4, damage: 9 } }`
  - shares: `{ youShare: 4, aiShare: 1 }` (your damage on a subsystem counts four times an AI's)
- Produces `perSide(tier) → 32 | 20 | 10`.
- Produces `createBattle({ war, attacker, at: [x, y, z], axis: [dx, dz], perSide, rand }) → battle`, with:
  - `teams[2]: { side, tickets }`
  - `capitals[]: { id, team, kind, role, size, pos, fwd, up, hull, hullMax, shield, subs[], alive, dying }`
  - `fighters[]: { id, team, kind, role, pos, vel, fwd, bank, hp, alive, mode, target, size }`
  - `bolts[]: { on, team, kind, x, y, z, vx, vy, vz, life, damage, mine }`
  - `phase`, `clock`, `over`
  - `setYou(team | null)`
  - `update(dt, you /* { x, y, z, vx, vy, vz, alive } | null */) → events`
  - `hit(from, to, damage) → { id, kind, at, size, down, sub } | null`: your shot, against the other team only
  - `targets`: the other team's live fighters, then the current phase's subsystems, as `{ id, at, vel, size, kind, sub }`
  - `info`
- Events:
  - `{ type: 'down', team, kind, at, mine }`
  - `{ type: 'hurt', damage }`
  - `{ type: 'sub', sub, kind, phase, at, mine }`
  - `{ type: 'phase', phase }`
  - `{ type: 'shield', down: true }`
  - `{ type: 'capital', id, kind, team, at }`
  - `{ type: 'arrive', team, kind, at }` (a respawn coming out of hyperspace)
  - `{ type: 'over', winner, why: 'flagship'|'tickets'|'clock' }`

The behaviour:

- **The lines.** Team A's capitals sit `lines` behind the centre along `-axis`, team B's along `+axis`, facing each other. They drift at 0.3 units a second and hold station.
- **Each fighter** re-picks its target about every 0.4 s, staggered by id:
  - fighters: the nearest enemy fighter, with a pull toward ones attacking a friend and toward you
  - interceptors: enemy bombers first
  - bombers: the defender's current-phase subsystem when they're on the attacker; the attacker's flagship's turrets as points when they're on the defender
- **Steering.** Lead pursuit at `FIGHTERS[kind].speed`, with the heading turned toward the wanted direction at most `turn` rad/s. On top of that:
  - capital avoidance: hull spheres from `galaxy/world.js`'s `HULLS`, moved into `wars.js` as `HULLS` so both read one copy, with a 2-second lookahead
  - separation from friends within 1.2
  - back toward the centre past `radius`
- **Modes.**
  - `engage`. Inside 3 units, head-on, it goes to `break` for 1.2–2.2 s, turning away. With an enemy on its tail inside 10 units and within 15°, it `jink`s on a random roll.
  - Bomber `run`. Inside 12 units of its subsystem, aimed, it fires a torpedo, then `extend`s 30 units, then loops back.
- **Fire.** Bursts of 3 bolts, `burst[0]` apart, then a `burst[1]` rest, while the target is within `range` and inside `cone`. Spread 0.02 rad.
- **Turrets.** Every capital turret fires `turbo` at a random enemy capital every 2–4 s, and `flak` at enemy fighters (you included) within 25 units every 0.5 s, 6° of spread.
- **Hits.**
  - Bolts are swept against: enemy fighters (radius `size * 0.7`), you (radius 0.3), enemy capitals' current subsystems (`r * size`) and enemy capital hulls (spheres).
  - A hit on a hull while the defender flagship's shield is up does nothing but flash.
  - A subsystem takes damage only in its phase.
  - AI torpedoes do `aiShare`, yours `youShare`.
  - All of a phase's subsystems down → `phase++`. After phase 3 the flagship starts `dying` (6 s), then `over`.
- **Deaths.** A fighter at 0 hp is `down`, and costs its team a ticket. With tickets left it respawns after `respawn` seconds, arriving near its own capitals. `no respawn without tickets`. A team on 0 tickets with no fighters left loses: `over` with why `tickets` (only the attacker can lose this way; the defender losing all fighters just keeps fighting with turrets). The clock reaching `BATTLE.clock` → the defender wins, why `clock`.

The steps:

- [ ] Write the failing tests:
  1. Spawn counts: `perSide` fighters a side, the side's capitals, each fighter's kind from its side's list.
  2. A lone fighter steered at a fixed target turns onto it, and fires only inside `cone` and `range`.
  3. A bolt swept through an enemy fighter kills it in `hp` hits, emits `down`, and takes a ticket.
  4. `only the defender's flagship has objectives`: `targets` subsystems belong to it alone.
  5. While both shield generators stand, a hit on the bridge does nothing. After both go: `phase` 2, a `shield` event, and the bridge takes damage.
  6. Phases advance only in order.
  7. `a dead player is not targeted`: `you.alive = false`, and no fighter's target is `you`.
  8. The player is targeted only by the other side: with `setYou(0)`, no team-0 fighter's target is you.
  9. `no respawn without tickets`.
  10. A battle with `rand` seeded and nobody playing ends within the clock: 720 s of `dt` 1/30 gives `over`.
  11. Your shots on a subsystem count `youShare` times an AI torpedo's.
  12. Performance: 1200 steps at `perSide` 32 run in under 4 s in Node.
- [ ] Run them. Expected: FAIL.
- [ ] Implement it, in this order: spawn, steering, fire and bolts, hits and phases, respawn and end. Run the tests after each.
- [ ] Run them all. Expected: PASS.
- [ ] Commit: "The Fleet Assault sim: fleets, fighters that dogfight and bomb, turbolasers, phases and tickets".
