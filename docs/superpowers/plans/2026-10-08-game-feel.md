# Game feel, every game: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Every game on the site answers a hit (a sound by force, dust, a shake, hitstop), forgives a press (coyote time, a buffer), eases its camera by dt, moves its secondary motion on springs, has a way out, and puts its feel numbers on the panel; the four worlds where the player hits things get props that scatter.

**Architecture:** One small kit lane lands three pure files (`lib/press.js`, `lib/spring.js`, `lib/ease.js`), grows two (`lib/three/feel.js`, `lib/sfx.js`), carries the closed car lane’s springs over, and fills a ratchet test from the audit. Six world lanes then work the games tier by tier at the edges (the rules’ events, the `.jsx` handlers, the smallest scene edits), merging a pull request a tier.

**Tech Stack:** three.js 0.186, Rapier through `src/lib/physics` (Tier 3 only), Web Audio through `src/lib/sfx.js`, Vitest. No new dependency.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-08-game-feel-design.md`; the findings it acts on, with every line number, are `docs/research/2026-10-08-game-feel-audit.md`. The first design’s Phase 1 pieces (`2026-10-08-one-feel-site-wide-design.md`, its plan’s Phase 1) are merged or merging and are used here, not repeated.

## Global Constraints

- The first plan’s Global Constraints, every line (the layers, no dependency, nothing lost, British spelling, files under 800 lines, draw calls no higher, the standing checks before a pull request, one pull request a tier, merge commits only, additive edits in files an open pull request touches).
- A shake, a fov change and a hitstop camera effect are off under `prefers-reduced-motion` (`lib/hooks.js`’s `prefersReducedMotion`); hitstop’s slower dt stays.
- Every k a game’s shake had is kept as its trauma value; every sound a game played still plays; a new sound is synthesised in `lib/sfx.js`.
- A rule change (i-frames in Lawn and the trench, a reset key) is named in the pull request as one.
- Feel wiring goes into `rules.js` (the event carries its magnitude), the `.jsx` handler and the smallest edit of `scene.js`; a scene file is never reformatted.
- Mario 64 and the Minecraft tribute keep their input and cameras.

## Review Focus

1. A hitstop that fires every frame on a held key must not freeze the game: `press.test.js` pins `take()` answering once; the HQ loops’ `timeScale` is already per hit (Task 1F.4 pins the loop rule with a test on a fake loop).
2. A press made while the tab was hidden (dt of seconds) must not fire on return: `createPress` drops a press older than `buffer` after one `ground()` with a dt over 0.25 s (Task 1F.1).
3. `play(name, { gain: 0 })` must schedule nothing audible and throw nothing; `gain` over 1 is clamped (Task 1F.3).
4. A spring stepped at 1/30 s with k 240 must not blow up: `spring.test.js` pins the semi-implicit step bounded at dt 1/30 for k up to 300 (Task 1F.2).
5. A game whose `feel` is `calm` must still run hitstop (the dt slows) but move no camera: `feel.test.js` pins both (Task 1F.4).

### Task 1F.1: A press that lands

**Files:** Create `src/lib/press.js`, `src/lib/press.test.js`.

**Interfaces (produces):** `createPress({ buffer = 0.12, coyote = 0.1 } = {}) → { press(), ground(onGround, dt), take() → boolean, pending → boolean, reset() }`; `createCooldownPress({ buffer = 0.12 } = {}) → { press(), ready(isReady, dt), take() → boolean, reset() }`; `pressGroups(press) → groups` (one group `press`: `buffer 0…0.3`, `coyote 0…0.3`). The clock is internal, advanced only by `ground`/`ready`’s dt; `take()` is true when the last press is within `buffer` and the ground was true within `coyote`, and consumes the press; a `ground()` with `dt > 0.25` forgets the press.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests**: a press then `ground(true, 0.05)` → `take()` true, then false; `ground(true, 0)` → press → `ground(false, 0.08)` → `take()` true (coyote), at 0.15 false; press → `ground(false, 0.1)` → `ground(true, 0)` → true (buffer); press → `ground(false, 0.2)` → `ground(true, 0)` → false; `ground(true, 0.5)` after a press → false; the cooldown press: press during not-ready, `ready(false, 0.05)`, `ready(true, 0)` → true; `pressGroups` reads and writes.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run** `npx vitest run src/lib/press.test.js` → FAIL. **Step 3: Implement.** **Step 4: Run** → PASS.
- [ ] **Step 5: Commit** `A press that lands: coyote time and a buffer, for every jump, hop and dash`.

### Task 1F.2: Springs

**Files:** Create `src/lib/spring.js`, `src/lib/spring.test.js`, `src/lib/ease.js`, `src/lib/ease.test.js`.

**Interfaces (produces):** `springStep(x, v, target, k, c, dt) → [x, v]` (semi-implicit: `v += (−k·(x − target) − c·v)·dt; x += v·dt`); `createSpring({ k = 120, c = 8, max = Infinity, dims = 1, x = 0 }) → { x (number or number[]), v, kick(dv), target(t), step(dt) → x, reset(), set({ k, c, max }) }`; `springGroups(spring, name) → groups`. `damp(k, dt) → 1 − exp(−k·dt)`; `approach(a, b, k, dt) → a + (b − a)·damp(k, dt)`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests**: at rest stays 0 over 2 s; `kick(1)` with k 120 c 8 peaks under 0.1 and is under 0.01 after 1 s; the same at dt 1/30 and 1/120 agree on the peak within 20%; k 300 at dt 1/30 stays bounded for 5 s; `max` clamps; `target(1)` settles to 1; `dims 2` kicks both; `damp(10, 0.1)` is `1 − e⁻¹` within 1e-9; `approach(0, 1, 10, 0.1)` the same.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run** → FAIL. **Step 3: Implement.** **Step 4: Run** → PASS.
- [ ] **Step 5: Commit** `Springs and an ease, pure, for every lean, squash and antenna`.

# Lanes 2A to 2F: the games (`claude/feel-<group>`, from `main` after 1F and Phase 1)

Each lane: the spec’s roster row is its list of games and specifics; the audit section for each game is its line numbers. Work **Tier 1 on every game, then Tier 2, then Tier 3**, a pull request a tier (Tier 3 one a world). Per game, Tier 1 is this checklist; the spec’s roster says which lines apply.

### The per-game Tier 1 checklist

- [ ] **Step 1: Hitstop real.** The loop steps with `feel.step(dt)` (or multiplies by `feel.timeScale(dt)`); a game without a feel makes one (`createFeel({ baseFov })`) in its scene and hands `feel.trauma`, `feel.hitstop`, `feel.punch` to its handlers; its heaviest hit gets `hitstop(60…90)`. Remove the file from `DEAD_HITSTOP`.
- [ ] **Step 2: One shake.** The game’s own shake lines become `feel.trauma(k)` with the k the audit lists, `feel.update(dt)` in the loop, the camera offset applied where the old one was; the old code goes. Remove from `OWN_SHAKE`. In dev, reduced motion: the camera holds still.
- [ ] **Step 3: Every event answered.** The event carries its magnitude from the rules (`{ type: 'bump', force }`: a bump speed × the mass the spec names); the handler calls `impacts.onHit(force, at)` (a `wireImpacts` in the scene: thud, dust, shake) or `play(name, { gain })`. Remove from `UNANSWERED`.
- [ ] **Step 4: Sound by force.** Every hit, bump, crash and land sound site takes the law’s gain and pitch (`hit(name, force)`), or `near` where it is by distance.
- [ ] **Step 5: A press that lands.** Each jump, hop and dash the audit names goes through `createPress`/`createCooldownPress` in the rules (the rules take `press.take()` where they read the flag; the handler calls `press.press()` on the key edge and `press.ground(grounded, dt)` each step). A rule test per game: a press 0.08 s before landing fires on landing; 0.08 s after leaving the ground fires. Remove from `NO_PRESS`.
- [ ] **Step 6: Eases by dt.** `min(1, dt·k)` and per-frame constants become `damp(k, dt)` with the k that gives the same feel at 60 Hz (`k_exp = −60·ln(1 − k/60)`). Remove from `LINEAR_CAMERA`.
- [ ] **Step 7: A way out.** Where the spec names it: R and the pad’s Back (or a free key where R is taken: Albuquerque’s is B and Y, as R runs a delivery and `games/pad.js` reads no Back) → the last safe spot (kept every 0.5 s when grounded and not colliding), under a second, a fade of 260 ms; i-frames where named, as a rule with a test.
- [ ] **Step 8: The panel.** `tune()` or `stage.tune([...feelGroups(feel), ...impactGroups(rules), ...pressGroups(press), ...its own])`.
- [ ] **Step 9: Check and commit** (the standing checks, the routes, the shots); commit `<Game>: hits answered, a press that lands, one shake`.

### Tier 2, per vehicle or walker

- [ ] A vehicle: `createVehicleFeel()` stepped from what the rules know (speed, forward acceleration, lateral acceleration or yaw rate × speed, steer, airborne, landing speed, hit gain), applied with `attachVehicleBody` to the body’s pivot; the old lag or sine goes; the panel gets `feelGroups`.
- [ ] A walker with a jump: `createSpring({ k: 120, c: 8, max: 0.3 })` kicked by `landingSpeed × 0.25` to `× 0.36` on the landing event (`× 0.05` peaks near 2%, too little to see; `0.36` gives a 16% squash at 8.9 m/s), applied as `scale.set(1 + s/2, 1 − s, 1 + s/2)` about the feet each frame; the sine squash goes.
- [ ] Check and commit `<Game>: its lean and its landing on springs`.
