# Battlefront 2017 pipeline, phase 1: the heroes on the game's skeleton, with what they hold. Implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The galaxy's playable and duelling heroes are the 2017 game's figures on the game's whole skeleton, moving with the game's own clips (a generic humanoid pack and one pack per hero), holding the game's hilts and hero blasters in the game's own weapon socket, and the saber duel plays on them end to end with the game's strikes, blocks and staggers.

**Architecture:** One skeleton, one clip library, many bodies, the way the game does it. `scripts/bf2017-skeleton.mjs` writes the `Walrus_HumanMale` rest skeleton once (from Luke) to `public/models/galaxy/bf2017/walrus.glb`; `scripts/bf2017-clips.mjs` turns the game's own clips (`web/anims/`, one glTF per clip on that skeleton) into packs, a generic humanoid one and one per hero, with the site's clip names mapped onto the game's by a pure, tested `src/lib/three/walrusClips.js`; a new loader `src/lib/three/walrus.js`, over a pure `walrusRig.js` that knows the game's bone and socket names, loads any 2017 body and drives it with the packs by bone name, returning the same figure object `crew.js` returns today. Nothing from the UAL or Meshy libraries touches a 2017 figure. Each hero is one skinned GLB assembled by `scripts/bf2017-import.mjs` (phase 0) from the manifest's body, head, hair and cloth parts at one LOD, with every joint kept. Hilts are ten small catalogue kinds parented to the rig's `Wep_Root` socket. The figure loaders' callers, the animator, gunplay and the combat rules do not change.

**Tech Stack:** phase 0's scripts and modules; `scripts/ual-bake.mjs`'s exported `contactWindow` and `rootTravel` only; three.js (`AnimationMixer`, `PropertyBinding` by node name, `SkeletonUtils.clone`); Vitest; Playwright through `scripts/anim-check.mjs`, `scripts/galaxy-check.mjs` and `scripts/glb-shot.mjs`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-battlefront-2017-asset-pipeline-design.md` (sections 3, 4, 6, 7; phase 1 of section 5; the owner's answer 5: the rig whole). Phase 0's plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-10-bf2017-phase0-tools.md`. The numbers: `docs/superpowers/evidence/bf2017-assets/inventory.md`.

## Global Constraints

- Everything in phase 0's Global Constraints (keys, paths, caps, the sequel list, the gates).
- **The rig is whole, the clips are the game's.** No bone is removed or renamed on a 2017 figure, in the skeleton file or in a clip pack. Meshy's skeleton, names and shared library, and the UAL sets, are not used for a 2017 figure.
- A hero GLB: plain cut from the LOD with ≤ 8,000 triangles for the body (LOD2 for most), parts at the same LOD, 1024 WebP colour, 512 WebP ORM, 1024 KTX2 normal where `scripts/ktx2.mjs`'s `verdict` says `convert` and WebP otherwise; under 4 MB with `hero: true`; `.lod1` at the LOD with ≤ 2,500 triangles and 512 maps. No animation inside a hero GLB.
- The clip packs under `public/models/galaxy/bf2017/`: `walrus.glb` (the rest skeleton, no mesh), `clips-humanoid.glb` (the generic set, under 2 MB), `clips-<hero>.glb` per hero (its combat and locomotion, under 1.5 MB each; Luke's 69 combat clips are 7.5 MB raw, so meshopt's animation quantisation and 24 fps must do it: measure and say the number). Each is the skeleton plus animations, no mesh.
- The game's names the site reads (from the Luke sample in `inventory.md`): body `Hips Spine Spine1 Spine2 Neck Neck1 Head LeftShoulder LeftArm LeftForeArm LeftHand RightShoulder RightArm RightForeArm RightHand LeftUpLeg LeftLeg LeftFoot LeftToeBase RightUpLeg RightLeg RightFoot RightToeBase`; fingers `{Left,Right}Hand{Thumb,Index,Middle,Ring,Pinky}{0..4}` (thumb 1–4); sockets `Wep_Root Wep_Muzzle Wep_Aim IK_Joint_LeftHand IK_Joint_RightHand`.
- `anim-check.mjs` at `--limit 0.15` passes on every world a hero stands on; no figure at bind pose.
- No sequel-era outfit: `luke_rotj_01`, `luke_farmboy_01`, `luke_hoth_01`, `luke_yavin_01` are fine; `palpatine_ep9_01`, `chewbacca_ep7_01` and anything with `ep7`/`ep9` are not (phase 0's `isSequel` refuses them).
