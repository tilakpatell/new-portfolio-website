# Battlefront 2017 pipeline, phase 1: the heroes, with what they hold. Implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The galaxy's playable and duelling heroes are the 2017 game's figures on the 2017 rig with fingers, holding the game's hilts and hero blasters, moving with the site's clips, and the saber duel plays on them end to end.

**Architecture:** Each hero is one skinned GLB assembled by `scripts/bf2017-import.mjs` (phase 0) from the manifest's body, head, hair and cloth parts at one LOD, its rig pruned and its bones renamed to the names the site's code already looks for, with the `core` and `sword` clip sets baked onto its own rest pose by `scripts/ual-bake.mjs --rig --into`. The hilts are ten small catalogue kinds; `createSaber` wears a hilt GLB in place of the procedural drum. The site's figure loaders, animator, gunplay and combat rules do not change; a one-line bone list and a grip row let them find the new bones.

**Tech Stack:** phase 0's scripts and modules; `scripts/ual-bake.mjs`; three.js; Vitest; Playwright through `scripts/anim-check.mjs`, `scripts/galaxy-check.mjs` and `scripts/glb-shot.mjs`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-battlefront-2017-asset-pipeline-design.md` (sections 3, 4, 6, 7; phase 1 of section 5). Phase 0's plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-10-bf2017-phase0-tools.md`. The numbers: `docs/superpowers/evidence/bf2017-assets/inventory.md`.

## Global Constraints

- Everything in phase 0's Global Constraints (keys, paths, caps, the sequel list, the gates).
- A hero GLB: plain cut from the LOD with ≤ 8,000 triangles for the body (LOD2 for most), parts at the same LOD, 1024 WebP colour, 512 WebP ORM, 1024 KTX2 normal where `scripts/ktx2.mjs`'s `verdict` says `convert` and WebP otherwise; under 4 MB with `hero: true`; `.lod1` at the LOD with ≤ 2,500 triangles and 512 maps.
- Baked clips per hero: the `core` set (7 clips) and the `sword` set (31) for a saber hero; `core` only for a blaster hero. Under 160 KB added per saber hero, 60 KB per blaster hero (the spec's section 4; the 2017 rig has about 70 bones after pruning against Meshy's 24).
- `anim-check.mjs` at `--limit 0.15` passes on every world a hero stands on; no figure at bind pose.
- The bone names the site looks for, which the import's rename must produce (from `src/lib/three/rig.js:127-144`, `actors.js:338`, `saber.js:79`, `src/lib/three/animator.js`): `Hips, Spine02, Spine01, Spine, neck, Head, LeftShoulder, LeftArm, LeftForeArm, LeftHand, RightShoulder, RightArm, RightForeArm, RightHand, LeftUpLeg, LeftLeg, LeftFoot, LeftToeBase, RightUpLeg, RightLeg, RightFoot, RightToeBase`. The 2017 rig's `Spine → Spine1 → Spine2 → Neck` chain maps to `Spine02 → Spine01 → Spine → neck` (lowest first; Meshy's `Spine02` is the one above the hips). Finger bones keep their 2017 names (`LeftHandIndex1` …): nothing in the site names them yet, and `rig.js` has no finger role.
- No sequel-era outfit: `luke_rotj_01`, `luke_farmboy_01`, `luke_hoth_01`, `luke_yavin_01` are fine; `palpatine_ep9_01`, `chewbacca_ep7_01` and anything with `ep7`/`ep9` are not (phase 0's `isSequel` refuses them).
