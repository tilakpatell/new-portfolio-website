# Battlefront 2017 pipeline, phase 1: the heroes on the game's skeleton, with what they hold. Implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The galaxy's playable and duelling heroes are the 2017 game's figures on the game's whole skeleton, moving with one shared clip library baked once onto that skeleton, holding the game's hilts and hero blasters in the game's own weapon socket, and the saber duel plays on them end to end.

**Architecture:** One skeleton, one clip library, many bodies, the way the game does it. `scripts/bf2017-skeleton.mjs` writes the `Walrus_HumanMale` rest skeleton once (from Luke) to `public/models/galaxy/bf2017/walrus.glb`; `scripts/ual-bake.mjs --rig walrus.glb --into clips-<set>.glb` bakes each clip set once onto it; a new loader `src/lib/three/walrus.js`, over a pure `walrusRig.js` that knows the game's bone and socket names, loads any 2017 body and drives it with the shared clips by bone name, returning the same figure object `crew.js` returns today. Each hero is one skinned GLB assembled by `scripts/bf2017-import.mjs` (phase 0) from the manifest's body, head, hair and cloth parts at one LOD, with every joint kept. Hilts are ten small catalogue kinds parented to the rig's `Wep_Root` socket. The figure loaders' callers, the animator, gunplay and the combat rules do not change.

**Tech Stack:** phase 0's scripts and modules; `scripts/ual-bake.mjs`; three.js (`AnimationMixer`, `PropertyBinding` by node name, `SkeletonUtils.clone`); Vitest; Playwright through `scripts/anim-check.mjs`, `scripts/galaxy-check.mjs` and `scripts/glb-shot.mjs`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-battlefront-2017-asset-pipeline-design.md` (sections 3, 4, 6, 7; phase 1 of section 5; the owner's answer 5: the rig whole). Phase 0's plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-10-bf2017-phase0-tools.md`. The numbers: `docs/superpowers/evidence/bf2017-assets/inventory.md`.

## Global Constraints

- Everything in phase 0's Global Constraints (keys, paths, caps, the sequel list, the gates).
- **The rig is whole.** No bone is removed or renamed on a 2017 figure, in the skeleton file or in the clip files. Meshy's skeleton, names and shared library are not used for a 2017 figure.
- A hero GLB: plain cut from the LOD with ≤ 8,000 triangles for the body (LOD2 for most), parts at the same LOD, 1024 WebP colour, 512 WebP ORM, 1024 KTX2 normal where `scripts/ktx2.mjs`'s `verdict` says `convert` and WebP otherwise; under 4 MB with `hero: true`; `.lod1` at the LOD with ≤ 2,500 triangles and 512 maps. No animation inside a hero GLB.
- The shared clip files under `public/models/galaxy/bf2017/`: `walrus.glb` (the rest skeleton, no mesh), `clips-core.glb` (the `core` set, 7 clips), `clips-sword.glb` (the `sword` set, 31), `clips-life.glb` (the `life` set). Each is the skeleton plus animations, meshopt-compressed, and the three together stay under 1.5 MB (250 joints at 24 fps; measure and say the number).
- The game's names the site reads (from the Luke sample in `inventory.md`): body `Hips Spine Spine1 Spine2 Neck Neck1 Head LeftShoulder LeftArm LeftForeArm LeftHand RightShoulder RightArm RightForeArm RightHand LeftUpLeg LeftLeg LeftFoot LeftToeBase RightUpLeg RightLeg RightFoot RightToeBase`; fingers `{Left,Right}Hand{Thumb,Index,Middle,Ring,Pinky}{0..4}` (thumb 1–4); sockets `Wep_Root Wep_Muzzle Wep_Aim IK_Joint_LeftHand IK_Joint_RightHand`.
- `anim-check.mjs` at `--limit 0.15` passes on every world a hero stands on; no figure at bind pose.
- No sequel-era outfit: `luke_rotj_01`, `luke_farmboy_01`, `luke_hoth_01`, `luke_yavin_01` are fine; `palpatine_ep9_01`, `chewbacca_ep7_01` and anything with `ep7`/`ep9` are not (phase 0's `isSequel` refuses them).

## Review Focus

1. The playable hero (`HEROES` in `src/components/galaxy/heroes.js`, loaded through `loadPartyFigure` in `src/components/universe/footScene.js` at `scene.js:696`) and the NPC duellist (`activity.js:368` through `crewFigure`) reach figures by two paths; both must hand a 2017 hero to the walrus loader. Task 4 wires both; task 7 proves both in the browser.
2. A figure whose parts each came with their own skeleton copy: after the import's merge the GLB has one skin and the body's joint count (248–260), or the clips drive one copy and the cape stays stiff. Task 5's import of every hero asserts it with a one-line check (`node -e` over gltf-transform: `skins.length === 1`).
3. A clip track naming a bone a given body lacks (Luke has 254 joints, Vader 250; a body without `LeftArm_Phys_01`): three's `PropertyBinding` warns and skips; the loader must filter tracks to bones the body has before making the mixer, so no console flood and no dropped frame. Task 3's loader test pins the filter.
4. `Wep_Root`'s orientation: the game's weapons are modelled for that socket, but its axes are not known until measured; a hilt that comes out pointing down or backwards is a socket-frame bug, not a model bug. Task 6 measures the socket's frame on Luke once, writes it to `walrusRig.js` as a constant, and tests that the blade's up vector in world space points away from the hand when the figure is at rest.
5. The duellist's reactions (`lib/ai/react.js`: `die.fwd`, `die.back`, `hit.head`, `hit.chest`, `die.blown`) and `activity.js:497`'s preload list name clips from the `life` and troops sets; a 2017 duellist that lacks one must fall back silently to the nearest it has (`hit.chest` for `hit.head`, `die` for `die.*`), not freeze. Task 3's loader maps the missing names and the test pins the map.

### Task 6: The hilt in the socket

**Files:**
- Modify: `src/lib/three/walrusRig.js` (`WEAPON_FRAME`), `src/components/galaxy/heroes.js:38-45` (`HILTS` rows gain `model: 'hilt<name>'`), `src/components/galaxy/surface/saber.js:105-140, 584-604` (`createSaber` wears the model and parents the gun to the socket; `dress` recolours only when there is no model), `src/components/universe/gunplay.js` (`createGunplay(fig, …)`: when `fig.sockets?.weapon` exists, the gun group is added to that bone with `WEAPON_FRAME` instead of the hand-and-`GRIP_FIX` placement; `GUNS.saber.build` unchanged)

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `WEAPON_FRAME = { quaternion: [x, y, z, w], position: [x, y, z] }` in `walrusRig.js`: the transform from the `Wep_Root` bone's frame to the site's gun frame (the gun's +y is the blade's direction, `gunplay.js:821-824`).   - `hiltFit(box: { min, max }, length: number) → { scale: number, bladeY: number }` (pure): the scale that makes the box's extent along the blade axis `length`, and `bladeY` = the scaled box's max along that axis. No recentring: the origin is the game's grip point.
