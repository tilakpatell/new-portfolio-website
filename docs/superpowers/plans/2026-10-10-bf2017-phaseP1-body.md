# Battlefront 2017 physics, lane P1: the body. Implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task, yourself, in order. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. Build directly; one judge pass at most before the PR.

**Goal:** The player on a level-pack world walks, sprints, crouches, climbs, steps, slides, jumps and falls with the 2017 soldier’s numbers, on Rapier’s character controller over lane P0’s shapes; everywhere else the walker runs untouched.

**Architecture:** The physics library that PR #781 built (`character.js`, `queries.js`, `groups.js`, `budget.js`, `zones.js`) returns from its branch as it was, library only. A rulebook builder reads the 13 `CharacterPhysicsData` records into `src/data/bf2017/physics/soldier.json` with sources. A pure `soldier.js` turns a row into the controller’s options and the pose speeds. `playerBody.js` puts the surface’s player on the controller when the world has an engine, writing the walker’s state back so nothing else on the surface changes.

**Tech Stack:** Node 22, Vitest against the engine, `src/lib/physics/world.js`, lane 0’s `scripts/lib/bf2017-ebx.mjs` when on `main` (else a local `loadAsset`/`deref`, forty lines), Playwright through `scripts/physics-check.mjs` (new).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-bf2017-physics-design.md` (§2, §6, §7, §8; “What is on `main`”, the #781 paragraph).

## Review Focus

1. **The pose’s capsule**: Rapier’s capsule `halfHeight` is the cylinder’s half-length, not the whole; stand 1.7 with r 0.3 is `halfHeight 0.55`, crouch 1.15 is `0.275`; `soldier.test.js` asserts both, and a crouch under a 1.3 m beam passes while a stand does not (against the engine).
2. **Deceleration gain −15**: the data’s negative gain means a stop over a frame or two, not a reversal; `accelFor` clamps the step so velocity never crosses zero on its own. Test: from 5 m/s with no input, three substeps reach 0 and stay.
3. **State written back**: `scene.js`, the HUD, the legs and the peers read `state.x/y/z/vy/grounded/speed/yaw/wading/air`; `playerBody.test.js` asserts every key the walker sets is set after a `step`, and `walker.test.js` stays green untouched.
4. **The engine failing to load** (offline, an old browser): `playerBody` falls back to `walk()` on the frame it learns, with no gap in the state; test with a `createPhysics` that rejects.
5. **A teleport** (`__surfaceDo('teleport')`, a quest’s move, the landing): sets the body outright and clears the knock; the next step does not fly from where it was.
