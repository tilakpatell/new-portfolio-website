# Handoff: Rapier as the body (lane one, the galaxy surface)

The design is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-rapier-body-ai-brain-design.md`, the plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-09-rapier-body-ai-brain.md`, the decision `docs/decisions/2026-10-09-rapier-as-the-body.md`, the evidence `docs/superpowers/evidence/rapier-body/`. Every figure on a galaxy surface, the player included, now walks on Rapier’s character controller, sees through its rays, and is hit by region through capsules set from its bones. The next lanes move the universe’s other worlds (Rick and Morty, Middle-earth, the rest with figures that fight or chase) onto the same modules, one world a lane, as the runtime’s migration went.

## Done

- **The physics library** (`src/lib/physics/`, each file tested in Node against the engine): `budget.js` (a per-frame query budget), `groups.js` (`GROUPS` with `character`, `hurtbox`, `zone` and the query-only `projectile` and `sight`; `filterOf`), `queries.js` (`ray`, `sweep`, `overlap`, `floorAt`, `project`; `undefined` when the budget refused, `omit` a set of bodies to pass over), `world.js` (a collider’s `sensor` and `tag`, a body’s `onEnter`/`onLeave`, `Body.attach`/`detach`, `tagOf`), `zones.js` (a sensor volume), `character.js` (`createCharacter`: the kinematic capsule on the controller, `move(intent, dt)` from the substep hook, `knock`, `jump`, `prev`, `teleport`, push-out of a solid it starts in), `hurtbox.js` (`REGIONS`, `capsuleBetween`, `createHurtboxes`), `cost.scenario.test.js` (the bench, under `npm run test:ai`).
- **The brain and the hits**: `src/lib/ai/mind.js` and `states.js` (the state machine: interrupts first, a rate with a phase, an intent held between ticks; the seven generic rows), `src/lib/combat/damage.js` (`resolve`, `KNOCK`, `STUN`, `WHERE`), `src/lib/combat/strike.js` (a blade swept as a Rapier cast; unused in this lane, see below), `src/lib/three/combat/hitboxRig.js` (bones to hurtboxes, `segments` for the plain maths, `blade()`, `debug()`).
- **The galaxy surface**: `surfacePhysics.js` (the world from `heightAt`, the walker’s solids, the floors, a floor at knee depth under water; `q` and `qb` the brains’ and the bolts’ queries; `seesThrough`; `toggle` for tagged solids and floors; `addSolid` through `createSolids`’s new `onAdd`), `playerBody.js` (the player on a character: `walkIntent` out of `walker.js`’s `walk`, the state synced back each frame, a direct write to the state carried into the body, the shore and the edge from the body’s own position), `hostileBodies.js` (a hostile’s character, rig, hurtboxes and mind; `drive(plan, from, dt)` from activity.js’s planner, `sync`, `saw`, `struck`, `knock`, `stun`, `dead`, `rate`), `activity.js` (the plan driven, the body read back in `sync()`, `hit()` through `resolve` with `where`, `rigs()`), `solids.js`/`aimShot.js`/`blaster.js`/`boltPlay.js` (a bolt and the aim as a ray through the world; `capsulesOf` by region, padded 8 cm), `saber.js` (the sweep against the regions; `where` on a hit), `scene.js` (`stepPhysics` once a frame in the walk and the ride; the physics tuning group under `?debug`; the dev hooks `__surfaceScene.physics()`, `hurtboxes(on)`, `quest(id)`), `tune.js` (`physicsGroups`).
- **The checks**: `scripts/hurtbox-sheet.mjs` (a picture of the hurtboxes over a world’s hostiles, with a frame’s counters), the cost bench, the smoke on Tatooine and Hoth after each surface task, `npm run lint`, `npm test`, `npm run build`, `node scripts/health.mjs --check --skip build`.

## Where the code differs from the design and the plan

- **The planner stays.** The spec’s `mind.js` was to wrap `hostileStep` inside its chase and attack states. The surface’s `hostileStep` and `duelStep` already decide hold, strafe, close, cover, flank, look and search; rewriting them as mind states would have been the gameplay rewrite the plan forbade. So activity.js plans as before and hands the plan to `hb.drive`; the mind decides what the planner can’t (stunned, dead) and names the mode from `saw`. `states.js`’s generic rows (patrol a route, chase a belief, search where it last saw you) are for a world without a planner.
- **The saber keeps `blade.js`’s arc sweep.** A stroke sampled between frames follows the swing’s arc; one Rapier cast a frame follows its chord and cuts an arc short. The sweep runs against the regions’ capsules read from the rig (`hitboxRig.segments`, `blaster.js`’s `capsulesOf`), so a hit says where without the cast. `strike.js` is built, tested and waiting for a melee that doesn’t swing (a bite, a fist).
- **The bolts stay on `bolt.js`’s capsule maths** for the same reason, with the solids through the world’s ray. `bolt.js` is untouched.
- **The player has no hurtboxes yet.** Their bolts hit you as before (`boltPlay.js`’s `personAt` capsule); the duellists’ blades too. The player’s regions are the next lane’s first task on the surface (`hitboxRig` over the party figure, `createHurtboxes` on `pb.c`).
- **The mate stays on the walker** (`walk(q, …)` in `stepWalk`), and so do the ground war’s soldiers, the Battlefront armies, the chase and the rides: they meet the physics world only as bolt solids. Each is a lane.
- **A light hit knocks but does not stun**: the flinch plays on the upper layer with the feet going, as before; a stun on every bolt would lock a trooper under fire. A heavy hit stuns 1.2 s and knocks 6 m/s.
- **Hit rates changed.** The one wide capsule (r 0.4 to 0.45) is now ten regions (chest r 0.2, head 0.12, limbs 0.06 to 0.09), padded 8 cm for the bolts and the blades. A stroke at the edge of reach that used to land on the fat capsule may now miss. Tune `hurtbox.js`’s `REGIONS` and `blaster.js`’s `HURT_PAD` by play; the sheet shows the regions over the figure.
- **Rapier loads on every surface now**, phones included (`WORLD_MB` already counted its 1.7 MB); the `?body=walker` switch is gone; the walker runs only when the engine fails to load.
- **Three numbers came from measurement**: the controller’s normal nudge 0.01 (at 1e-4 a run along a heightfield catches about once a second), the push-out of a figure placed inside a solid (the controller never resolves a starting penetration), and the shore and the edge judged from the body (the drawn state is a substep behind and slipped past the deep-water line).

## Left

- **A real chip’s frame time.** The cost is measured in Node (2.07 ms a frame for twenty figures: `evidence/rapier-body/cost.json`); `scripts/perf-probe.mjs surface` on this container runs on software WebGL and says nothing about the body. Run it on the owner’s desktop and put the row in the evidence README.
- **The hurtbox wire in a real browser.** `scripts/hurtbox-sheet.mjs endor` gets six rigged troopers out, drawn (six rigs) and counted (`evidence/rapier-body/hurtboxes-endor.json`), but the wire over the figure is not seen in the software-WebGL screenshot; `hitboxRig.test.js` proves the meshes follow the bones in Node. Open `?debug#/galaxy/endor/surface` on a real screen, start the bunker quest, call `__surfaceScene.hurtboxes(true)` and look; if nothing shows, check the wire group’s parent (the scene) against what the post chain draws. Tatooine’s and Hoth’s first quests spawn inside a zone or not at once; a `zone` option on the script (as `surface-shot.mjs` has) would follow a figure inside.
- **Feel.** The player’s walk was checked by the smoke (a canvas drew, no console error on Tatooine and Hoth) and by `playerBody.test.js`’s numbers, not by hand on a real screen: this container has no GPU. Walk a hut, jump a crate, fall off a ledge, wade on Kashyyyk, ride and dismount; compare against `main` if anything feels off. The jump buffer and the coyote time go through `walkIntent` and a forced `jump` on the character; a roll and the crowd’s shove reach the body as a teleport the next frame.
- **The hostiles were watched only through the sheet** (a figure out, rigged, its mind `patrol`). On the trooper quests: a trooper loses you behind a hut, searches, finds you (`__surfaceScene.physics().figures` shows each mind).
- **The next worlds**: Rick and Morty (`c137`), Middle-earth’s towns, then the rest. Each: its solids into a `createSurfacePhysics`-like builder (the walker’s `createSolids` is the surface’s; a town has its own), its figures on `createCharacter` with `createHurtboxes` and the rig, its planner handed to `drive`, its hits through `resolve`.
- **`strike.js`** waits for its first user; **`zones.js`** likewise (a rancor’s bite, a town’s alarm line).
- **The physics tuning group** reads its counters when the panel draws them; it is a snapshot, not a live graph.
