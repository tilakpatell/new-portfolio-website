# Handoff: the combat revamp

The design is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-08-combat-revamp-design.md`, the plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-08-combat-revamp.md`, the audit behind both `docs/research/2026-10-08-combat-feel-and-offline-motion.md`. Each lane adds its section here when it merges; the next lane reads this first.

## Lane A: the look and the reticle

### Done

- **The look** (`src/runtime/look.js`, tested; exported from `src/runtime` too). `createLook({ host, win, onTurn(dx, dy), onButton(which, down), onLock(on), sensitivity, drag, mode, active })` → `{ attach, detach, request, release, set(mode), locked, refused, mode, prompt }`. `onTurn` takes **radians** (positive dx: the pointer went right), scaled by `sensitivity` (lock: yaw 0.0022, pitch 0.0018 rad/px) or `drag` (each world passes its old drag numbers). Modes `'lock' | 'drag' | 'touch'`; `defaultMode({ coarse, safariNoMouse, kept })` and `senseLook(win)` pick one (touch on a coarse pointer whatever was kept; else the visitor's pick under `tp-look`; else drag for Safari on a Mac, lock for the rest). `SPIKE` 60 px per event, `RELOCK` 1.25 s, `TP_LOOK`, `PROMPT` ("Click to look · Esc to release").
  - Review Focus 1: a refused lock (`NotSupportedError` asks again plainly; any other rejection, or a synchronous throw) throws nothing, keeps the prompt, and the drag goes on turning; after a refusal a still click is the left button, so the mouse can still fire.
  - Review Focus 2: each locked move is clamped to ±60 px, and the first move after locking is dropped.
  - Two things the plan didn't list, both tested: `active()` (the universe's canvas is the map's too: the look only works on foot, and lets go of the lock at the next move once you're back in the ship) and `refused` (the universe skips the shot on the click that asked for the lock).
  - In drag mode the left button is reported only for a click that barely moved (6 px), so a drag never fires.
- **Where a shot goes** (`src/lib/combat/aim.js`, tested, plain arrays). `aimPoint(ray, solids, targets, { min = 1.5, max = 120 })` → `{ at, target, dist }` (`target` is the capsule object; its `ref` is the world's thing); `assist`, `friction` (0.55 or 1), `lead`, `ASSIST`, `coneFor({ coarse, mode })` (a mode `'touch'` counts as coarse), and `rayCapsule(o, d, a, b, r)` exported for anyone who needs a ray against a body. `assist`'s cap bounds one call, so a mouse's 0.01 rad cap means a shot 0.015 rad off is pulled 0.01, not all the way: the test uses an uncapped cone to show the pull alone.
- **The reticle** (`src/runtime/hud/Reticle.jsx`, its rules `reticle.js`'s `reticleState`, tested; both from `src/runtime/hud`). Four ticks that close with `tight`, the `--hud-bad` colour for 180 ms on a hit, a `--hud-accent` ring on a lock; drawn in `hud.css`, no text, pointer-events none. A world skins it by `--hud-ink`, `--hud-accent`, `--hud-bad` on its own class.
- **The galaxy surface** (`scene.js` plus two small files beside it, so `scene.js` grew by about fifty lines, not the whole wiring):
  - `surfaceLook.js`: the controller on the canvas; `pressLook` (tested) maps the buttons onto the flags F, C and the touch buttons already set: left `buttons.fire` (a gun's shot is queued too, so a quick click still shoots; a saber's press is noted and the stroke goes on release, held for the heavy one, the touch Swing's path), right `ads` with a gun and `buttons.block` with a saber. The page hears `{ type: 'look', mode, locked, prompt }`.
  - `aimShot.js` (tested): `aimDir` is the camera's ray, from level with the figure (nothing between the camera and your back is aimed at), against the shootables as capsules and Lane B's `boltSolids` (walls, props, the ground) as the solids, bent by `assist` with the look's cone; `fire()` and the burst's next shot take that direction from the eyes' point, so the muzzle's bolt goes to what the crosshair is on. `lookFriction` slows `turn()` over a target with a gun up.
  - `look(dx, dy)` keeps its pixels for the page's touch pad and calls `turn(dx, dy)` in radians; the emote wheel under the lock is pointed at by summed movement, and the turn waits while it's open.
  - `GalaxySurface.jsx`: the kit's `Reticle` in place of `.surface-crosshair` (shown when the old crosshair was, and whenever a gun is up in the walk; tight with the sights; the hit flash; the lock ring); the kit's `Prompt` for the look, bottom middle, a click on it locks too; the Menu's **Look: Click to lock / Drag** (`input.lookMode`, kept under `tp-look` for every world).
- **The universe on foot**: `universe/scene.js` makes the controller with `active: onFoot` and the foot's old drag numbers; the mouse's on-foot drag now goes through it; a click still fires through the map's own `onUp` (and under the lock), except the click that asked for the lock. `footScene.js` gained `turn(dx, dy)` in radians (`look` in pixels calls it). `UniverseMap.jsx` shows the kit's Prompt top middle while on foot and not locked (`lookLock()` on the scene asks for it).
- **Rick and Morty** (`RmWorld.jsx`): the controller in an effect on the canvas; a mouse is its, a finger still the old drag. Total Rickall's shot is `onButton(0)` (a click in drag mode, the left button under the lock). `RmHud.jsx`: the kit's `Reticle` in place of `.rm-crosshair` (the lime ring on whoever's in the sights) and the look's Prompt when nothing else is asked.
- **The guide** (`guide/pages.js`) and the street's first hint say "Click, then the mouse: look round (Esc lets go)"; the galaxy's rows say the left and right buttons.

## Lane B: bolts that obey the world

### Done

- **One bolt step for every shot** (`src/lib/combat/bolt.js`, tested). `createBolts({ pool })` → `fire(spec)`, `step(dt, world)`, `live()`, `clear()`. Each frame a bolt flies `speed · dt` and that segment is tested against `world.solids(a, b)`, the bodies' capsules and the raised blades, nearest first; solids win a tie, so a muzzle pressed into a wall stops at the muzzle and never hits its shooter (Review Focus 3). A bolt never hits its owner, a body of its own side or a body whose `allies` lists its side; a `ghost` (a battle's tracer) only stops at solids. A bolt marked `deflect` meeting a blade of another side is turned back along its line, becomes the blade's (`side`, `owner`) and flies home: the `deflect` event. `BOLT_SPEED` = 90 m/s is every blaster's.
- **The geometry, for Lane C's blade**: `segSeg(a, b, c, d) → { s, t, dist }` and `segCapsule(a, b, ca, cb, r) → { t, at } | null` are exported from `bolt.js`, plain arrays, the names and signatures the plan gives.
- **How enemies shoot** (`src/lib/combat/accuracy.js`, tested): `spread`, `scatter(dir, rad, rng, min)`, `freshAim`, `shotStep` (two hits in a row and the next goes wide, once), `missBy(range)`, `FIRST` 2.5, `MOVING` 1.6, and `lead(target, vel, from, speed)`.
- **The bolts drawn** (`src/lib/three/combat/bolts.js`, tested): `createBoltMeshes(parent, { pool })` → `sync(live)`, `flash(at)`, `update(dt)`, `dispose()`; one instanced mesh, coloured per bolt.
- **The galaxy surface on the step.** `galaxy/surface/solids.js` (tested with a fixture world) is the surface's `world.solids`: the walker's circles and boxes standing from `base` to `top` (a null top is tall), and the ground through `groundAt`. `blaster.js` is now a caller (`fire` aims along the eyes' line at the first solid or body and the bolt leaves the muzzle for it; `enemy`, `shoot`, `tracer`, `update(dt, { bodies, blades })` → events). `boltPlay.js` (new, beside `scene.js`) builds the bodies each frame (you, the mate, the ground soldiers through `groundWar.bodies()`, the quests' or the battle's figures) and hands the step's events to the scene: `yours`, `hurt`, `mate`, `other`, `deflect`, `landed`. What a shot hits is decided when the bolt gets there; the impacts land then too.
- **Gone**: blaster.js's `along` (hitscan), the tracer's separate 140 m/s, the 0.45 dice for soldier-on-soldier shots in `scene.js`'s shooters, `grazes` (the mate is a body like any other now), the deflect cone decided at fire time.
- **Enemies**: `fight.js` and `hostiles.js` lead a mover they can see (`lead` at `BOLT_SPEED`); `boltPlay.enemy` fires only along a clear line from the muzzle (the 2.5 s of intuition still moves them, it no longer shoots through a wall), widens the first volley at a fresh target (× `FIRST`, fresh again after 6 s without a shot at you) and throws the shot after two hits wide. The ground soldiers' bolts go into the scene's one pool (`groundScene.js`'s `loose`), land where they land (`groundWar.bolt(e)`), and a bolt going within 2 m keeps a head down (`ground/bolts.js`'s `nearBy`). `ground/bolts.js`'s `createBolts` is the same step on its own pool for the headless scenario (`npm run test:ai -- ground`), with a 2D line of sight for walls.
- **The universe on foot** (`universe/foot.js`, tested): `footBodies` (you, the mate, the troops as capsules), `footSolids(obstacles, R)` (the planet's sphere and each obstacle as a column; `top` if it has one, else twice its radius and never under 2.2 m), `BOLT`, `velOf`. `march` leads a mover, holds fire with a solid between (looks again in 0.3 s), and its first shot at you is × `FIRST` wide. `footScene.js`'s bolts fly the step (`obstacles()` and the loose props as solids); its own meshes and first-person hiding are as they were. `bolt`, `fly` and `pass` are gone from `foot.js`.

### Left

- **The raised blade is a stand-in.** `boltPlay.guardOf` puts a fat segment (r 0.6) across your front while `saber.deflecting(...)` says the block is up, so bolts from the front and the sides are turned as the old cone did. When Lane C's `blade.js` has the real segment, pass it as `ctx.guard` in `scene.js`'s `stepBolts` instead. (Lane C has: `saber.guard()`, below.) The deflect costs guard and sparks as before; a turned bolt that hits someone deals 2 (`stepBolts`' `yours`, `deflected`): Lane D tunes it.
- **`lead` lives in `accuracy.js`.** Lane A's `aim.js` wasn't merged when this was written; when it is, `fight.js`, `hostiles.js` and `foot.js` can take its `lead` and this one goes.
- **The player's aim point** is `blaster.aim` (the eyes' line against `solids` and the targets' capsules) until Lane A's `aimPoint` + `assist` land: then `fire()`/`shot()` pass its `at` and `blaster.fire` only launches.
- **Not on the step yet**: the chase's bike cannon (`chaseScene.js` uses `blaster.fire`'s predicted target for its hit; its bolt flies and hits nothing it tracks), the assault's own count (`assault.js:695` still rolls; its bolts are ghosts that walls stop), Total Rickall and the duel dimension (Lane E). The universe's own shot still goes to the auto-lock (Lane E).
- **The streak cap on foot** isn't wired: `march` has the first-shot telegraph but not `shotStep` (the universe's troopers don't hear whether they hit).
- **The galaxy's solids are the walker's shapes, not the meshes.** The plan said to raycast `placer`'s solid meshes; `solids.js` tests the circles and boxes `placer` already makes from them (with their `top` and `base`), which is what you walk into, costs no three.js raycast and runs in Node. A prop whose collider is wrong is wrong for walking too; fix it there.
- **Obstacle heights on foot are guessed** from their radius; a builder's solid could carry `top` (`solidsOn`) when one looks wrong.

## Lane C: strokes from clips, hits from the blade

### Done

- **The sword set** (`scripts/ual-bake.mjs --set sword`): all thirty-one of UAL2's sword clips, whole-body, one file each at `public/games/meshy/ual-sword.<name>.glb` (518 KB in all, 12–36 KB a clip; off the mannequin by 0.0002 rad at worst). Each clip's extras, read at runtime as `clip.userData`:
  - `contact: [t0, t1]`: seconds into the clip where its blade lands. Found by `contactWindow` on the blade's tip (a metre out of Luke's right fist along the hand's +z, where gunplay's grip puts the blade), counting only frames when the tip is ahead of the hips, then widened 60 ms either side and kept inside `[0.05, duration − 0.05]` (rounded inward). Timing on the hand alone, as the plan first said, put the window on the wind-up: these clips swing the blade round behind the back as fast as they cut.
  - `root: [[t, dx, dz]…]`: the root's travel from where the clip starts, at 30 a second, from `UAL2_RM.glb`, in metres on Luke, the figure's own +x (its left) and +z (ahead).
  - `rootHips`: Luke's hips over his toes (0.92 m), the height the root was measured on; a figure scales the travel by its own over this.
  - Names (`clipLibrary.js`): `sword.a`, `.b`, `.c`, `.combo` and the recoveries `sword.a.rec`, `sword.b.rec`; `sword.light.a`–`.d`, `sword.light.combo` and `.a.rec`–`.c.rec`; `sword.heavy.a`–`.d`, `sword.heavy.combo` and `.a.rec`–`.c.rec`; `sword.aerial.a`, `.a.rec`, `.b`, `.combo` (loops), `.idle` (loops); `sword.block`, `sword.dash`, `sword.pound` (the ground pound), `sword.uppercut`. `sword.heavy` is an alias of `sword.heavy.a`, which the Death Star's duel plays under its old name. `ual-saber.glb` and `saberBody.js` are gone.
- **The blade** (`src/lib/combat/blade.js`, tested): `createBlade({ r = 0.12, keep = 8 })` → `{ push(base, tip, t), sweep(capsules, { steps }), clash(other), crosses(a, b, within), history(), clear(), r }`. The sweep turns the blade between frames (its direction slerped about the hilt), not across its chord, so a fast arc can't step over a thin body. Its geometry is `bolt.js`'s `segSeg` and `segCapsule` (one copy); a body is `blaster.js`'s `capsuleOf`, as the bolts see it.
- **The trail** (`src/lib/three/combat/trail.js`, tested): `createTrail(parent, { color, length })` → `{ sync(frames, on), color(c), dispose(), mesh }`, drawn from `blade.history()`; one per blade, so a staff and a pair trail both.
- **The stance table as clips** (`combatRules.js`, tested): `STANCES[id].strokes = [{ clip, speed, damage }…]` (the combo, in turn); `HEAVY = { hold, clips: ['sword.heavy.a'…'d'], speed, damage, breaks }`; `DIRS = { up, left, right, rise }` (`{ clip, damage }`: W overhead `sword.heavy.a`, A `sword.b`, D `sword.a`, S `sword.uppercut`); `BLOCK_CLIP`, `DASH_CLIP`; `STRIKE` (1 m, where a stroke's step stops short of its lock); `PARRY.window` 0.25 s. `strokeFor(stance, { last, now, dir, heavy, combo })` → `{ clip, speed, damage, lunge, heavy, kind ('combo' | 'heavy' | 'dir'), i }`; `rootScale(travel, dist, lunge)` → how much of the clip's step to take. `swingPose`, `arcHit`, `nextSwing` and `saberRules.js`'s `SWINGS`, `swingPose`, `nextSwing`, `arcHit`, `SABER.reach/half/damage` are gone; `throwAt` and `deflects` stay until B's deflect lands. `lungeTo` stays (nothing in the saber calls it now).
- **The saber** (`galaxy/surface/saber.js`, the scenario in `saber.test.js`, which runs in `npm test`): a stroke plays its clip full-body on the figure's animator (`fig.play`) for the legs and hips, and saber.js lays the same clip's chest and arms over gunplay's at the same time, so it works after `gp.set` without touching the frame loop. The root's travel moves `me` (scaled to land `STRIKE` short of the lock up to the contact window, capped at the stance's lunge times the perk; the clip's own after). The figure turns to the lock over the first 40% of the clip, eased. Inside the contact window the sword arm turns toward the lock's chest (with no lock, toward a chest a stroke's length ahead) by at most 0.25 rad. Every lit frame pushes each blade's segment; inside the window each blade sweeps the targets' capsules and `hit(target, damage, at, { heavy })` lands once a stroke a target, `at` on the blade. A stroke may be cut by the next 50 ms after its contact window ends. Moving at the click (`stand`'s move over 0.3), the legs keep walking and the clip isn't played on the full layer. The block lays `sword.block`'s arms held at 32% of the clip, where the blade is up across. Without its clip yet, a stroke is timed as one (0.6 s, contact 0.2–0.4) and swings nothing.
- **The block** (Step 2b): held on C (or the Block touch button), the block clip's arms held at 32% of `sword.block`, the blade up across; a press of any length shows it for `PARRY.window`. The bolts see the real blade now: `saber.guard()` → `{ id: 'you', base, tip, r: 0.3, side: 'you' }` while the block shows (else null) is what `scene.js`'s `stepBolts` hands Lane B's step as `ctx.guard`, in place of `boltPlay.guardOf`'s stand-in (which nothing calls now: it can go). So a bolt whose flown segment passes through the held blade is turned home by B's `deflect` event, and one that passes beside it lands, cone or no cone; the sparks, the sound and the guard's cost are B's `deflect` callback as it was. `r` is 0.3, wider than the blade's 0.12: a streak a hand across against a held block. An enemy's melee into the raised blade still spends the guard and deals nothing, and a block pressed within `PARRY.window` (now 0.25 s) before it is a parry, as before (decided at the swipe, through `deflecting`, until Lane D's duellists land on contact).
- **scene.js**: `swing()` reads the way held (W, A, D, S against the camera) and passes the lock; the old lunge (`state.lunge`, `stepLunge`) is gone. `saberHit` takes the sweep's point as it came. DEV: `__surfaceScene.swing({ heavy, dir, lock })` makes a stroke now.
- **Online** (`protocol.js`, tested): the walk packet's `arms` gains a sixth item, the stroke's clip name (validated `sword.*`, at most 32 characters); an older packet's five read as before. `STANCES_SEEN` is `combatRules.STANCE_IDS`. `peers.js` plays the clip the packet names, again whenever it names another (a chained stroke); peers' strokes stay cosmetic (`targets: []`).

### For Lane D (duellists on this saber)

- **An enemy figure**: give it a gunplay saber and `createSaber(gp, { color, hilt, stance, parent, fig, sound })`, where `fig` is the rigged figure (`{ bones, hipsY?, play? }`: `activity.js`'s crew figure as it is). Each frame, in this order: the figure's update, `saber.stand(dt, now, move)`, the figure's `after`, `gp.set(...)`, then `saber.update(dt, now, { forward, up, me, targets, hit })`. `me` is the duellist's own `{ x, z, yaw }`: the stroke's root travel and its turn to the lock write into it. `targets` are what it may hit (for a duellist, you: `{ holder, fig: { tall }, spec? }`); `hit` is called once a stroke a target.
- **A stroke**: `saber.swing(now, { heavy, dir, lock, lunge, clip })` → the stroke or null (busy). `clip` plays a named one in place of `strokeFor`'s pick (what `duelStep`'s `stroke` would hand it).
- **The contact window** is `saber.swinging.contact` (`[t0, t1]`, in the clip's own seconds); the clip's time is `(now − swinging.t0) × swinging.speed`. That's what `duel.js`'s `you.swinging = { contact, t }` wants, and your parry window anchors to it (the block pressed within `PARRY.window` before their `contact[0]`).
- **The blades**: `saber.blades` (lib/combat/blade.js's, the main first; a staff or a pair has two). `saber.blades[0].clash(theirs.blades[0])` → `{ at }` for the clash; their `history()` for anything else.
- **The table's shape**: `STANCES[stance].strokes[i] = { clip, speed, damage }`, `HEAVY.clips`, `DIRS[dir].clip`, `BLOCK_CLIP`, `DASH_CLIP`; every name is a `clipLibrary.js` key.
- `heldBlade.js` now passes its figure to `createSaber` (that's all I changed there); its `targets: []` is yours to fill.
