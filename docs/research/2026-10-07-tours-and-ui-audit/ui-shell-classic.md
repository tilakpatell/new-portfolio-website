# UI audit: the shell and the classic pages

Read-only audit of tilakpatell.com's shell (nav, view switch, guide, command palette, tour, toasts, world gate, footer) and its classic pages (home, experience, projects, project detail, résumé, contact, travel, changes, 404, the welcome). Every file the brief named was read in full, with the lazy stylesheets the pages import, the helpers behind the labels (`lib/palette.js`, `lib/view.js`, `lib/tour.js`, `guide/routes.js`, `guide/pages.js`, `tour/steps.js`, `tour/briefs.js`) and the universe panel where it duplicates the shell. Counts come from greps over that set; contrast ratios from a script (`scratchpad/wf1/contrast.py`, WCAG 2 relative luminance).

House style, as the repo states it (`docs/PICKUP-PROMPT.md:54`, `.claude/skills/autopilot/SKILL.md:70`): British spelling, curly quotes, plain sentences, sentence case, no runs of exclamation marks.

Paths are relative to the repo root. Line numbers are from the files as read on 2026-10-07.

## 1. What is good: the baseline to keep

These are the pieces the rest of the site should be brought in line with. They are the "one house UI" reference.

1. **The token layer** (`src/index.css:10-57`). Semantic colour tokens (`--bg`, `--surface`, `--surface-2`, `--border`, `--border-strong`, `--text`, `--text-body`, `--muted`, `--accent`, `--accent-text`, `--btn-bg`, `--btn-ink`), one radius token per shape (`--r-card`, `--r-panel`, `--r-btn`, `--r-chip`, `--r-photo`), one card shadow (`--shadow-card`, line 38), one easing family (`--ease-out/smooth/spring/in`, lines 41-44), two fonts, and gutters that scale at one breakpoint (lines 49-67). Every theme, dark mode and the dark scope only override these (lines 69-342). This is exactly right and is the model for everything below.
2. **The button family** (`src/index.css:430-453`). `.btn` is 44px tall, `.btn-sm` 36, `.btn-lg` 50; `.btn-primary` and `.btn-ghost` are the only two fills; the radius comes from `--r-btn`. It is used everywhere it should be: 24 uses of `btn btn-ghost btn-sm` and 7 of `btn btn-primary btn-sm` in the secondary components alone.
3. **The type roles** (`src/index.css:399-454`): `.display`, `.title`, `.eyebrow`, `.lead`, `.label`, `.mono`. `SectionHeading` (`src/components/ui.jsx:50-63`) composes eyebrow → title (`mt-3`) → lead (`mt-4`), and is the right way to open a section.
4. **The page header rhythm.** Home (`src/pages/Home.jsx:125-160`), Projects (`src/pages/Projects.jsx:106-119`), Contact (`src/pages/Contact.jsx:151-162`) and Changes (`src/pages/Changes.jsx:100-111`) all open the same way: `pt-[calc(var(--nav-h)+40px)] md:pt-[calc(var(--nav-h)+72px)]`, eyebrow, display title `mt-6`, lead `mt-6`/`mt-7`, buttons `mt-9`, and sections at `py-14 md:py-20`. That is the rhythm.
5. **Card and panel** (`src/index.css:472-493`): one surface, one border, one radius token, one hover (`a.card:hover`, line 483).
6. **One global focus ring** (`src/index.css:380-384`) and **one reduced-motion rule** (`src/index.css:888-899`).
7. **Shared pieces that are actually shared**: `ViewSwitch` is one component used in the bar and the phone menu (`src/components/Nav.jsx:388,457`); `KeyTable`/`Keys` draw keys for the guide and the tour alike (`src/components/guide/KeyTable.jsx`, used at `src/components/tour/Tour.jsx:174`); `ScriptToggle` is the one language switch (`src/components/ScriptToggle.jsx:230-233`); the nav degrades by a declared order (`src/components/navFit.js:8`).
8. **The copy voice** in `src/components/tour/steps.js`, `src/components/tour/briefs.js` and the achievement descriptions (`src/components/Achievements.jsx:11-253`): short sentences, curly quotes, British ("colour" at `steps.js:23`, "neighbourhood" at `Achievements.jsx:191`, "kilometres" at `:191`), no hype. The tour's "Stuck? Press ?" (`steps.js:29`) and the offer's "New here? A quick look round" (`src/components/tour/TourHost.jsx:134-135`) are the tone to match.
9. **Icons** come from one set (`react-icons/ri`) and 53 of 80 icon instances are `h-4 w-4`; `RiQuestionLine` means "guide" in all three places it appears and `RiCompass3Line` means "tour" in all three.
10. **Tailwind spacing is mostly on the 4px grid**: the top ten classes by use are `mt-6`, `mt-3`, `gap-2`, `gap-3`, `mt-4`, `mt-1`, `mt-5`, `mt-8`, `mt-2`, `gap-4`.

The problems below are almost all cases where a later piece did not use one of these ten things.

## 4. Findings

Severity: **high** = a reader or a thumb is hurt, or the brand voice splits; **medium** = the kit forks; **low** = polish.

### 4.1 Spacing rhythm

**S1 · medium · classic.** The page header top offset differs by page: `+40/+72` on Home, Projects, Contact and Changes (`src/pages/Home.jsx:125`, `src/pages/Projects.jsx:106`, `src/pages/Contact.jsx:151`, `src/pages/Changes.jsx:100`); `+32/+52` on Experience (`src/pages/Experience.jsx:75`); `+28/+48` on a project (`src/pages/ProjectDetail.jsx:33`); `+40/+64` on the résumé (`src/pages/Resume.jsx:86`); `+40` only on the 404 (`src/pages/NotFound.jsx:28`); `+48` on Travel (`src/pages/Travel.jsx:48`); `+48px` on the feed error (`src/components/feed/feed.css:50`). Six offsets for "the first thing under the nav". Fix: one token `--page-top: calc(var(--nav-h) + 40px)` (72 at `md`), a `.page-head` class, every page uses it.

**S2 · medium · classic.** Section rhythm is `py-14 md:py-20` on Home (`src/pages/Home.jsx:190,221,260,293,311`) but `py-8` and `pt-10` and `pb-24 pt-16 md:pt-24` on Projects (`src/pages/Projects.jsx:127,134,138`), `mt-10 md:mt-12` / `mt-14 md:mt-20` / `py-16 md:py-20` on a project (`src/pages/ProjectDetail.jsx:69,85`), `pt-16 md:pt-24` and `pb-24 pt-20` on Experience (`src/pages/Experience.jsx:143,275`), `py-16 md:py-24` on Travel (`src/pages/Travel.jsx:72,165`), `pb-28` on Contact and Changes (`src/pages/Contact.jsx:167`, `src/pages/Changes.jsx:139`), `pb-24 pt-14 md:pb-32 md:pt-24` for Home's close (`src/pages/Home.jsx:343`), `pb-20` on the 404 (`src/pages/NotFound.jsx:28`). Fix: two classes, `.section` (`py-14 md:py-20`) and `.section-last` (`pb-24 md:pb-32`), nothing else.

**S3 · medium · shell.** Corner insets for floating UI: guide button `right: 16px; bottom: 22px` (`src/styles/extras.css:958`), guide panel `right: 16px; bottom: 74px` (`:968`), guide nudge `right: 68px; bottom: 18px` (`:1000`), tour offer `left: 16px; bottom: 22px` (`src/components/tour/offer.css:6-7`), world-gate pill `right: 68px; bottom: 23px` (`src/components/worlds/worldgate.css:33-34`), language pill `bottom: 22px` (`src/styles/extras.css:208`), toast `bottom-5` = 20px (`src/components/Achievements.jsx:337`), scroll saber `bottom: 6px` (`src/index.css:670`), world gate card `12px` all round (`worldgate.css:6-8`). Nine numbers for one edge, and 18/22/23 are three spellings of one intent. Fix: `--edge-x: 16px; --edge-y: 22px; --edge-stack: 52px` and derive every corner from them.

**S4 · low · shell.** Half-step Tailwind classes off the 4px grid (11 kinds, section 2.2), and `h-[52px]` for the nav bar (`src/components/Nav.jsx:380`) with `md:h-14`, while `--nav-h` is 68/76 (`src/index.css:56,65`) and the phone menu hard-codes `calc(12px + 52px + 12px)` (`src/styles/extras.css:379`). The bar's height lives in three places. Fix: `--bar-h: 52px` (56 at `md`), `--nav-h: calc(var(--bar-h) + 16px)`, menu padding from `--nav-h`.

**S5 · low · shell.** Floating-panel paddings in odd rem steps (`0.9rem 0.9rem 0.75rem 1.1rem`, `1.1rem 1.25rem 1.25rem`, `0.65rem 1.25rem`, `0.55rem 0.35rem 0.55rem 0.8rem`, `1.1rem 1.15rem 1rem`, `1rem 1.1rem 1.05rem`, `0.6rem 0.7rem`; `src/styles/extras.css:969-971,1000`, `src/components/tour/tour.css:17`, `src/components/worlds/worldgate.css:11`, `src/styles/lazy/commandpalette.css:19`). **S6 · low · shell.** Floating widths: tour card 340 and 390 (`src/components/tour/tour.css:16,39`), tour offer 330 (`offer.css:12`), guide nudge 300 (`src/styles/extras.css:1000`), guide panel 460 (`:968`), world gate 30rem = 480 (`worldgate.css:9`), theme dropdown 19rem = 304 (`src/components/Nav.jsx:211`), toast `max-w-md` = 448 (`src/components/Achievements.jsx:341`), palette 640 (`src/styles/extras.css:295`). ### 4.2 Type scale

## 5. A one-house rule set (what the findings add up to)

1. **Tokens are the only source of a number.** Colour, radius (`--r-*` plus `--r-sm`), shadow (`--shadow-card/float/dialog`), z (`--z-page/nav/float/sheet/dialog/cover/top`), duration (`--t-fast/base/slow`), easing (the four), corner edges (`--edge-x/y`), page top (`--page-top`), measure (`--measure`), lift (`--lift`). A hard-coded `px`/`rem`/number in a stylesheet or a Tailwind `[…]` needs a comment saying why.
2. **Type is eleven sizes**: `--fs-xs 0.8125`, `sm 0.875`, `md 0.9375`, `base 1`, `lg 1.0625`, `lead 1.125–1.3 (clamp)`, `title 1.65–2.4`, `display-3`, `display-2`, `display-1`, and nothing under 12px. Weights 500/600/700 (680 on display). Line-height 1.6 body, 1.5 small, 1.25 titles, 1 controls.
3. **Spacing is the 4px grid**: Tailwind whole steps only; sections `py-14 md:py-20`; last section `pb-24 md:pb-32`; `.page-head` top; card padding 24 (`p-6`), compact 20 (`p-5`), control padding 12/16.
4. **Six interactive kinds and no more**: `.btn` (primary/ghost, sm/lg, on-photo), `.chip` and `.chip-btn`, `.seg`, `.link` and `.link-quiet`, `<Kbd>`, `<CloseButton>`. Every control is 44px on a coarse pointer, 36 minimum otherwise, 8px apart.
5. **Two label styles**: `.eyebrow` (mono caps) and `.label` (sans). Two heading styles per page: `.display-*` for the page and its hero feature, `.title` for sections.
6. **One `<Layer>`** with three kinds and one backdrop; **one `<Notice>`** for corners; every floating surface has a visible title and a `<CloseButton>`, and Esc closes it.
7. **One icon per meaning**, from `icons.js`, at 16/20/24.
8. **One word per thing**, kept in a small glossary module: *the universe* / *Universe* (switch), *the classic site* / *Classic* (switch), *the pages*, *the guide*, *the tour*, *colours* (never theme, scheme, color), *Start over*, *the Death Star*, *Esc*, *⌘K* / *Ctrl K*, *Download the PDF*, *See X* for pages, *Open X* for demos, *Copy email address*.
9. **Copy**: British spelling (colour, modernisation; "program manager" is the title's own spelling and the one exception), curly quotes, sentence case, one sentence per toast, no Oxford comma, a noun after "this" and "it".
10. **Dim by colour, never by opacity below 0.55, never grayscale on text.** Muted text stays ≥4.5:1 on every theme (today's floor is 4.67:1, Claude on `--surface-2`).
