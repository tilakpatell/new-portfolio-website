# Game feel, every game, audited against Bruno Simon’s habits

Date: 2026-10-08. Read-only, six readers over every playable thing on the site, one rubric: the step model, the input, the movement, the camera, the feedback on a hit, the physical reactions, the fail and respawn, the tests, the dev hooks. Paths are under `src/components/` unless they start with `lib/`, `runtime/` or `pages/`. Line numbers are from `main` at dd21c069 (the evening of 8 October). The design that acts on this is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-08-game-feel-design.md`.

## What he does (the habits this measures against)

From `2026-10-06-bruno-simon-folio.md` and `2026-10-08-folio-2025-physics-terrain-streaming.md`: every hit makes a sound whose volume is `clamp((v − vMin) × k)²`, pitch drawn from a range, one of several variants, throttled at 100 ms; the car’s secondary motion comes from real springs (suspension, an antenna), never a sine; controls are analog and forgiving (steering ramps and recentres, a coasting drag, must stop to reverse, auto-unflip after a second, R resets, H honks and jumps); the camera follows only the ground plane, eased, and pulls back with speed; props are light (0.02 to 1.5 against the car’s 40), asleep until touched, and scatter; a frame clamp at 1/30 s and a time scale of 2 give it an arcade pace; every number is on a panel.
