# The Star Wars worlds' buildings: an audit

2026-10-07. Every building on the galaxy's seventeen walkable worlds (`src/components/galaxy/surface/`), judged against Wookieepedia's pictures of the place as it is on screen.

## How

- **Inventory**: each world's sites (`sites/*.js`, made whole by `siteOf`) read for every structure placed, 110 world-and-kind pairs, each traced to what draws it: a Meshy model (`catalog/made.js`, `library.js`, `outer.js`, `clonewars.js`), a Sketchfab model (`catalog/*.js`) or code (`props/*.js`).
- **Renders**: each model on its own (`scripts/glb-shot.mjs`) and each code-built prop on its own (`scripts/preview/prop-shot.html`, new: a prop drawn as glb-shot draws a model), four views each; in-game shots (`scripts/surface-shot.mjs`) for some landmarks.
- **References**: Wookieepedia contact sheets per building (`scripts/galaxy-refs.mjs refs`, into the git-ignored `lab/refs/img/`), with better pictures searched out of the file namespace where the page's were interiors, comics or Lego.
- **Judging**: one agent per world group scored quality (geometry, textures, artefacts) and accuracy (silhouette, proportions, materials, colour) out of 10 and gave a verdict; a second agent per group tried to refute each verdict, checked each remake's picture and crop full size, and set a priority. 88 of 110 verdicts went through that second pass (the rest are marked unverified).

Verdicts: 61 tweak, 9 skip, 17 keep, 23 remake.

- **remake**: a new Meshy model from the named picture: the spec is in `scripts/meshy-galaxy-audit.mjs` (the audit lane), ready to run.
- **tweak**: a few lines of code, no Meshy (a tint, a scale, a scan, a yaw).
- **keep**: good enough.
- **skip**: not a building, or not one model (a walkable deck, a city, a lattice).

## Remakes, by priority

Run with the account that has the credits (about 33 a building: 3 for the lift, 30 for the model):

```
MESHY_API_KEY=$MESHY_API_KEY_ACC_3 MESHY_TASKS=scripts/meshy-galaxy-audit-tasks.json MESHY_REVIEW=lab/meshy/audit node scripts/meshy-galaxy-buildings.mjs lift <kind …>
# look at lab/meshy/audit/<kind>-lift.png, then: models, fetch, sheet
```

Then each kind needs its catalog entry (`made: 'meshy'`, in place of a Sketchfab one where there was one) and its line in `public/cc0/README.md`; the integration column says what.

All 23 were lifted and modelled on 2026-10-07 (759 credits). The kept ones are in their own catalog group, `src/components/galaxy/surface/catalog/audit.js`, listed on one line of `public/cc0/README.md`; the outcome column says what happened to each. Two came out as kinds of their own, because their old kind is shared with another world: `moscantina` (Nevarro keeps `cantina`) and `lothtower` (Yavin keeps `lookout`).
