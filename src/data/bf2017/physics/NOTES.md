# The 2017 game’s physics rulebooks: what is by hand, and why

Every number in these files has a `<key>_source` sibling naming `<asset>#<Type>.<Path>`, or sits under a row marked `source: "hand"` with a line here.

## materials.json (lane P4: surfaces)

Built by `scripts/bf2017-materials.mjs` from a level’s `MaterialGridData` (`data/Levels/MP/<Level>/<Level>/materialgrid_win32`), for the material indices the level’s meshes’ Havok shapes declare (`web/physics.jsonl`’s `materials[].index` on the records the map manifest names). Keyed by level; Hoth (`hoth_01`) first, 17 materials and 156 pairs.

- **How a tag finds its row.** A shape’s packed material declaration carries the index in bits 12..19 (the exporter’s `PhysicsExport.cs`; lane P0 writes it as the collider `tag`). The grid’s `MaterialIndexMap[index]` is the row (256 entries; an undeclared index maps to row 0, the default’s, and `DefaultMaterialIndex` is 0). `materials[i].row` keeps it.
- **Pairs** are keyed `"<surface>,<striker>"` and read from the striker’s row of `InteractionGrid` first, the transpose if that cell is empty (Hoth’s grid differs from its transpose in 330 of 24,649 cells).
- **`name` is by hand** (`nameSource: "hand"`; `nameFrom` the evidence): the material names are not in the data, so a surface is named by the rule in `nameFromEffect` from the blaster’s effect on it (`FX_Impact_Blaster_Metal` → metal, `_RockSnow` → rock snow, `_FFloor_PineDry` → pine floor), and a generic effect with a metal scorch (`Decal_Metal_Blaster`) reads “generic metal”.
- **`by` is by hand** (`source: "hand"`): the strikers’ indices, each named from its pairs’ effects on Hoth: blaster 5 (`FX_Impact_Blaster_<Surface>`), heavy 37 (`_M`), strong 119, ion 146, fast 197 (`_M_Fast_`), bowcaster 202, thermal detonator 145, impact grenade 149, saber throw 126, the soldier’s foot 3 (`FX_FootStep_Soldier_<Surface>`), a walker’s foot 82 (`FX_FootStep_ATST_<Surface>_Decal_01`). The projectiles’ own blueprints carry their material; lane P2’s `projectiles.json` can confirm these.
- **Index 14, Hoth’s most used after 0 in the survey, is metal, not snow**: its pair with the blaster names `FX_Impact_Blaster_Metal` and `Decal_Metal_Blaster`. Snow is 28 and 29 (`FX_Impact_Blaster_Snow`, `Decal_Snow_Blaster`); only 28 has the soldier’s footprint (`FX_FootStep_Soldier_Snow_Decal_01`), 29 a step and no print. 91 is rock under snow, 45 concrete, 144 pipe, 15 and 16 strong and weak-spot metal, 208 invulnerable.
- **Speed bands**: every effect relation on Hoth has a single band, 0 to 10,000 m/s. What makes a bolt and a grenade look different is the striker’s material (5 against 149), not the speed; the bands are kept, and `impactOf` picks by speed when a level has more than one.
- **Physics properties**: only four materials on Hoth carry `MaterialPropertyPhysicsData` (11, 73, 74, 141), and of Hoth’s shapes only 141 (restitution 1, friction 0 with modifier 1). `frictionOf` reads a zero friction as “the engine’s”, scaled by the modifier; with none, the engine’s (`src/lib/physics/materials.js` says which).
- **On walker worlds** (no level pack, so no Havok tags yet), `src/components/galaxy/surface/sites/ice.js`’s `materials` is a hand stand-in: the ground snow (28), a box (a wall) metal (14), a circle (a rock) rock snow (91). Lane P0’s numeric tags replace it where a pack is loaded.
- **Effect families** (`familyOf` in `materials.js`, by hand): the site has six impact looks (snow, metal, sand, rock, wood, generic), chosen from the game’s effect name.
